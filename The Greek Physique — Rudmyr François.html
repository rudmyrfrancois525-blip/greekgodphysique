Nutrition FAQ

How to eat for a lean Greek physique: protein, carbs and healthy fats, aiming to stay around 10% body fat.

How much protein do I need?

Aim for 1.6 to 2.2 g of protein per kg of body weight each day. At 80 kg that is about 130 to 175 g. Chicken, eggs and salmon cover most of it, and whey protein helps fill any gap.

Why do carbs matter for this physique?

Carbs fuel hard training and speed up recovery, which keeps muscles full and workouts strong. Rice, sweet potatoes, corn and fruit are my main sources.

When should I eat carbs?

Have a meal with carbs and protein 2 to 3 hours before training, and another within a few hours after. Keep the rest of the day lighter on carbs and rich in protein.

Which healthy fats should I eat?

Avocado, salmon, eggs and a little butter. Fats support hormones and joints, but they are calorie dense, so measure them. Roughly 20 to 30% of daily calories is a good range.

How do I stay around 10% body fat?

Keep calories close to maintenance and weigh yourself at the same time each week. If the trend moves, adjust portions in small steps of about 100 to 200 calories. Keep protein high and keep lifting so you lose fat, not muscle.

Is 10% body fat realistic to maintain?

For most men it is a lean, defined look with visible abs, and it is sustainable with steady habits. Going much lower is harder to hold and can hurt energy and recovery.

General guidance, not medical advice. Adjust it to your own body and ask a professional if needed.
